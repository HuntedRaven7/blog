## Why I love Gentoo Linux

### What is a Gentoo?
Gentoo is a source based Linux distro what that means is you have to compile all/most programs by source, now don't worry there are some packages like rust and the kernel that have binary versions that you don't have to compile but there is the choice and that is what is important, having choice to build software how you want is so cool to me, I have built a full [package factory in Github](https://github.com/HuntedRaven7/gentoo-ing-packages) because I can to be honest, I find it's great fun to be able to get as close as possible to the source of building packages.

### Why does Gentoo matter?
The big reason it matters is for the reason of choice! Yes Gentoo can be a difficult adventure for most but I find the approach I am taking to be the easiest for me that will of course vary per person BUT I find having choices with Gentoo to be really important, and at the end of the day it's YOUR computer so you should be able to do as you please install whatever you want with however way you want to do it.  

### Why are you using Gentoo?
Well I don't like the idea of having a middle man in the way to get my software I feel like I should be able get everything straight from the maker, be it Bootc or anything else and I also have the choice to build software for my machine so it's special to me it feels like a whole differant beast compared to others, I also really like the history of Gentoo with the whole idea of ports taken from BSD and such it's really cool you should take a look into it!
