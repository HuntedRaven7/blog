## The Gender Journey

### The Start Of The Flight/Rant
In my 19 years (So far) I have done lots of thinking and thinking with research about who I am. It has been quite an interesting journey for someone like me who didn't have a care in the whole world before about anything before.

If you want an idea of my past I was just a standard kid with Autism for my time everyone was into stuff like Minecraft and of course I was so invested in that. I used to play with my friends on that game for so many hours I was obsessed. Nothing would stop me from hopping on the game not school or anything that needed to be done. Pretty standard right?

Well I come to find later on in life that I thought I was Non-Binary but it never quite sat right with me something always felt off with that. Now I know the reason why it is because I am Transgender. More on the reason why it felt off (Of course there is nothing wrong with being Non-Binary) I always felt a bigger connection with Women my whole life and never knew why. I now know of the reason why and it was because in my mind I wanted to be a Woman.

### Little Robin Waking Up
I always wanted to wear more "Girly" clothing but that at the time was not the right thing to do when I was younger. As a Male at birth you want to be apart of the boys group in your class when you are young so you feel a part of a group. Especially being Autistic you want to feel normal even if you don't understand why and for a great point I didn't know why I wanted to fit in as well until I learned the great truth that I was Autistic. 

This phase of wanting to be "Normal" lasted for about until hit High School then the great collapse happened "Covid Lockdown" little did I know that this was going to be the greatest thing for me. This time was the first that I started so to speak wake up to the real truth that something was "different" with me. I thought nothing of this change in me like how most would but for me it stuck around big time.

I first thought I was Transgender to be honest but then my mind thought "No that can't be right". So I just used Non-Binary as a placeholder nothing for me to be really proud of to be honest just something to tell others that I am different. The thought of me being Transgender stayed with me for a very long time but I didn't think anything of it.

### The Little Baby Bird Flying
I didn't think anything of me possibly being Transgender for about two or so years after my first little encounter with the idea but at the start of 2026 the thought came back. I of course thought nothing of it since I was Non-Binary "There is no way that I am Transgender I am just Non-Binary" I thought. More and more the year progresses (As of today writing is Tuesday 23 of June 2026) I noticed that the thought kept coming back so in the second month of the year I gave the thought real time to bake. I kept going over memories and feelings coming back to mind and I realized that yes I am Transgender.
